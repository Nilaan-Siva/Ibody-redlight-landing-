About Red Light Therapy

What is red light therapy and how does it work?

Red light therapy (also called photobiomodulation) uses specific wavelengths of red and near-infrared light that gently penetrate the skin and are absorbed by your cells. This is thought to support the mitochondria — your cells' energy producers — which may help with skin renewal, recovery and circulation. It's warming, relaxing and completely non-invasive.

What are the benefits of red light therapy?

People use red light therapy & body contouring to support their weight-loss goals, healthier and more radiant-looking skin, eased muscle soreness, post-workout recovery, and relaxation. Because it supports circulation and cellular energy, many clients describe feeling refreshed and rejuvenated afterward. Individual results vary.

How many sessions do I need to see results?

Red light therapy is cumulative — it works best with consistency. Most people start with 2–3 sessions per week and notice the biggest difference over several weeks. That's exactly why our 4-session package is the most popular way to keep your momentum going.

Is red light therapy safe? Are there side effects?

Red light therapy is non-invasive and uses no UV light, so there's no tanning or burning. It's generally very well tolerated with minimal side effects, and we provide eye protection. If you have a medical condition or take photosensitizing medication, please let us know at your consultation first.

What does a session feel like and how long does it take?

It's relaxing and gently warming — you simply lie back and let the light do the work. Sessions are quick with zero downtime, so you can head right back to your day afterward.

About Vibration Plate Therapy

What is vibration plate therapy (whole-body vibration)?

You stand, sit or hold a pose on a platform that vibrates rapidly. Those vibrations make your muscles contract and relax many times per second — a bit like a passive workout — which can help activate muscles, boost circulation and support lymphatic flow.

What are the benefits of the vibration machine?

Whole-body vibration is popular for improving circulation, activating and toning muscles, supporting recovery, and stimulating the lymphatic system. It's a low-impact way to get the body moving — and a perfect warm-up before your red light session.

Why do you pair vibration therapy with red light therapy?

The vibration plate gets your circulation going and primes the body, so your full-body red light session feels even more effective. Together they make one complete reset — movement, circulation and cellular support in a single visit.

Is vibration plate therapy safe for everyone?

For most healthy adults it's gentle and low-impact. If you're pregnant, have a heart condition, a recent injury or surgery, or an implanted device, please check with your doctor and let us know before your session so we can keep you safe.
